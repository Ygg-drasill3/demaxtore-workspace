# Sprint 7B — Product Readiness Verdict

## Question

Can DeMaxtore identify growth bottlenecks, improve conversion rates, and optimize commercial performance using operational data already generated by the platform?

## Answer

**YES**

## Rationale

1. **Commercial funnel** — Nine-stage RFQ→shipment funnel with counts, conversion %, drop-off %, and timing from existing workspaces (no FSM changes).

2. **Conversion & drop-off analytics** — Dedicated endpoints expose where RFQs die and which transitions leak most (`/api/growth/conversion`, `/api/growth/dropoffs`).

3. **Buyer activation** — Scored and classified buyers (Cold → Power Buyer) from RFQ/order/shipment/activity data.

4. **Supplier performance** — Win rates, selection rates, and FreightIQ contribution per supplier organisation.

5. **Category & route intelligence** — Revenue and volume by `productCategory` and by freight lane (reuses Sprint 6B route engine).

6. **Repeat customers** — 30/90/365-day repeat buyer and revenue metrics.

7. **Lost opportunities** — Detects quoted-but-not-selected, PO-without-order, and similar leakage with estimated lost goods and FreightIQ margin.

8. **Growth dashboard** — `/operations/growth` consolidates funnel, activation, suppliers, categories, routes, repeats, and trends.

9. **Control Tower** — Six additive `growth.*` alerts via existing scan pipeline.

10. **No automation scope** — Read-only intelligence; no CRM, campaigns, or outreach.

11. **Access control** — All `/api/growth/*` routes ADMIN-only; buyers receive 403.
