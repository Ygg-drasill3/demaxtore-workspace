# Demo Video Script — DeMaxtore (3 minutes)

**Format:** Screen recording + voiceover (no camera).  
**Audience:** VP Procurement, import managers, COO at mid-market food / FMCG importers.  
**Environment:** `yarn demo:seed` · buyer `demo.buyer@demaxtore.com` · admin `admin@demaxtore.local`

---

## Opening (0:00 – 0:20)

**Visual:** `/welcome` landing page → click Sign in → ABC Foods buyer shortcut.

**VO:**
> Global sourcing still runs on email, PDFs, and spreadsheets. DeMaxtore is a B2B trade operating system — one workspace for every RFQ, auction, purchase order, and shipment. Let me show you how ABC Foods Germany runs a full import programme in one place.

---

## Act 1 — Sourcing (0:20 – 1:10)

**Visual:** Buyer dashboard hero → RFQ list → open `DEMO-RFQ-ABC-001`.

**VO:**
> ABC Foods opens their command center and sees live auctions, pending actions, and a guided first-trade checklist.  
> Their Q2 pantry restock RFQ covers pasta, tomato paste, flour, and juice — four line items, multiple invited suppliers.  
> Quotations arrive in the workspace — comparable, timestamped, auditable. No more chasing attachments in Outlook.

**Visual:** CommodityBid list → `DEMO-CB-ABC-001` closed auction.

**VO:**
> For commodities like tomato paste, they run a sealed CommodityBid reverse auction. Suppliers bid blind; the lowest valid offer wins. The full bid history stays in the workspace for compliance.

**Visual:** SmartContainer home `DEMO-MC-ABC-001` · BulkContainer `DEMO-BC-ABC-001` (quick pan).

**VO:**
> Mixed retail loads use SmartContainer — build a mixed FCL from catalog SKUs. Bulk programmes use BulkContainer for tonne-scale flour and pulses. Same platform, different procurement models.

---

## Act 2 — Award to execution (1:10 – 2:00)

**Visual:** PO list → `DEMO-PO-ABC-001` → order workspace → shipments list.

**VO:**
> When ABC awards pasta to Alpine Pasta Works, DeMaxtore spawns a purchase order and order workspace automatically — no re-keying.  
> FreightIQ compares forwarder sailings inside the order. Select a sailing and a shipment workspace is born — origin, destination, and PO reference linked from day one.

**Visual:** Shipment portfolio or shipment workspace `SHP-ORD-DEMO-RFQ-ABC-002-00000000`.

**VO:**
> Buyers track in-transit cargo alongside documents and exceptions. Suppliers see the same shipment from their portal — role-scoped, not a blind copy-paste.

---

## Act 3 — Operations & close (2:00 – 2:45)

**Visual:** Sign in as admin → Control Tower `/operations`.

**VO:**
> DeMaxtore operations teams run the Control Tower — open alerts for late PO acknowledgements, ETA drift, and container pricing delays. Resolve or escalate before the customer calls.

**Visual:** Resolve one alert · Exception Hub quick glance (optional).

**VO:**
> Exceptions map to owners and SLAs. The platform is built for operators, not just buyers — because imports break in the gaps between sourcing and delivery.

---

## Closing (2:45 – 3:00)

**Visual:** Return to landing or login with product pillars.

**VO:**
> DeMaxtore — one workspace, one timeline, one state machine. From first RFQ to final delivery. Book a pilot with your DeMaxtore contact, or ask for sandbox access today.

**End card text:** `demaxtore.com` · **B2B Sourcing & Import OS**

---
